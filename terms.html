2. What TopoXY does

TopoXY converts coordinates between GPS (WGS84) and six national grids (Romania Stereo70 and others listed in the app), shows points on a map, measures distance, bearing, area and perimeter, and estimates elevation and slope.

6. Important: results and your responsibility

Conversions use standard published EPSG transformations without national correction grids, so results can differ from official values by about 1 to 4 metres depending on the country (the app shows the expected accuracy). Elevation and slope are estimates from Mapbox terrain data. GPS position depends on your device.

TopoXY is a working aid, not a certified surveying instrument. Do not use it alone for cadastre, property boundaries, construction, legal documents or navigation where an error could cause harm; check results with a licensed surveyor or official sources.
